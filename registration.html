Registration

CMSB 2026 is an "FLoC associated workshop" with the following registration dates:
- Early bird until June 1st
- Late until July 13th

https://www.floc26.org/registration

Registration of all conferences/workshops will be centrally managed by FLoC, including our conference dinner, so please follow these instructions for CMSB:

  1. In the "Role" section
    • Select the appropriate: "Regular"/"Student"/"IEEE member"/...
  2. Select "Workshops Week 2" (and any other conference you might be attending)
    • Select "Computational Methods in Systems Biology (CMSB) || July 24-25"
  3. In the "Lunches" section
    • Select "July 24" and "July 25"
  4. In the "Workshop Dinner" section
    • Select "Workshop Dinner || July 24"
    • If your financing body has problems with a detailed "Dinner" item, please read the "Important Notes" section
Total registration fee (lunches, coffee breaks, and workshop dinner) should be approximately:
- Regular: 280 EUR
- Students: 210 EUR
